Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Neon Defense by Squatch Squad Studios collects and how it is used. It covers two versions of the game: the Android app on Google Play, and the website game at kwkoch.github.io/NeonDefense-.

Android app (Google Play)

The Android app does not ask you to sign in, does not collect or share personal information, and does not show ads. Your game progress is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The app can optionally send you local reminder notifications on your phone. It only asks for notification permission if you tap the Remind me button, and those reminders are scheduled on your device without any server involved. The app contains a link to this policy and a way to send feedback by email; if you use it, we receive whatever you choose to write in that email.

The sections below, from Sign-in through Deleting your data, describe the website version only.

Website: Sign-in (Google via Firebase Authentication)

You must sign in with Google to play. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We receive your Google account name, email address and a unique user ID (uid). We use these only to identify your account and show who is signed in. We do not sell this information.

Website: Cloud saves (Google Cloud Firestore)

Your game progress (levels, upgrades, energy and a progress score) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your user ID so you can play on any device. Only you can read or change your save. A copy is also kept in your browser's local storage so the game works offline.

Website: Advertising (Google AdSense / H5 Games Ads)

This site and game show ads through Google AdSense, including H5 Games Ads (interstitial and optional rewarded ads). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites.

  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Opt out of personalized advertising: Google Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Other data

Our hosting (GitHub Pages) and Google's services may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and request times. We do not run our own analytics.

Deleting your data

Use Delete account in the game's Account section to permanently delete your account and cloud save. Clearing your browser data removes the local copy.

Children

Neon Defense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.

Contact

Questions or requests: email info.squatch.squad.studio@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/KWKoch/NeonDefense-/issues.
